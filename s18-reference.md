## Traits that need a manual action (set them up at the start, or they're effectively off)

- **Blackthorn**: put a unit on the dark-red blood-pool hex on your side as the sacrifice (it uses a slot and counts for traits; it returns after combat).
- **Sprykin 3+**: drag a Sprykin unit onto the BFF and hold ~2 seconds to make it the rider (`dh`). The BFF doesn't use a slot.
- **Riftbeast 3+**: drag the Alpha Mark onto a Riftbeast unit.
- **Elderwood 3+**: grants placeable plants (Stonebark Tree, Lifebloom) that go on the board.
- **Taric (Emerald Aspect)**: drag an ally onto Taric to pair them.
- **Primal 2/4, Coven, Draven (Bounty Seeker), Kha'Zix evolution**: pop up choices you must make.
- **Ivern (Greenfather)**: seeds make certain hexes grow bonuses — mind positioning.

## Traits (breakpoints: effect)

- **Adaptor** 2/3/4: +25/35/50% of whichever is higher of AD/AP; ability form changes accordingly.
- **Blackthorn** 2/4/6: sacrifice the unit on the blood-pool hex; team gains +175/350/600 HP, and Blackthorn units gain extra stats based on the sacrifice's role/star level/cost (+30% at 4, +60% at 6). Measured: 2★ AP-tank Alistar → +31% HP +22 armor/MR; 1★ 5-cost Gnar → +44% AD +25% AS.
- **Blossom** 3/5/7/9/11: members +10% HP, +12/30/45/50/100% AD & AP; empowered Wisps after combat; from 5, every shop has a Wisp; at 7, buying Wisps refunds gold.
- **Brawler** 2/4/6: team +120 HP, Brawlers +25/40/65% HP.
- **Coven** 3/4/5/7: earn Essence from kills / lost rounds (2/2/3/10 per kill, 18/25/32/60 per loss); redeem rewards from 40.
- **Defender** 2/4/6: team +12 armor/MR, Defenders +25/60/120 armor/MR.
- **Elderwood** 3/5/7/9/11: placeable plants; 3: Stonebark Tree + Lifebloom, 5: a second tree and bonus HP, 7: Deepwood Protector, 9/11: plants become 2★/3★.
- **Executioner** 2/3/4: members gain Precision (abilities can crit) +15% crit; at 3 enemies also bleed 30% as true damage, at 4 40%.
- **Fae** 2/4: damage/healing/shielding attracts Pixies; each Pixie gives Fae units +5/8% AD & AP and heals 2.5/4% at low HP; at 4, after 7 Pixies they become gold Pixies (give gold).
- **Flora Fatalis** 1/2: takedowns restore 10 mana; at 2 also heals the lowest-HP ally for 8% max HP.
- **Hunter** 2/3/4/5: +20/30/45/65% AD; +10% damage amp after 3 seconds on the same target.
- **Inferno** 2/3/5/7: damage burns (1/1/3.5/4.5% HP per second) and applies 33% Wound; from 3, 1/2/4 shop slots ignite after combat, rolling a champion one cost higher.
- **Invoker** 2/3/4/5: team +1/1/2/2 mana regen, Invokers +2/3/5/8.
- **Juggernaut** 2/4/6: team +4/6/8% damage reduction, Juggernauts +20/33/45%.
- **Lunar** 2/3/4/5: Lunar units and adjacent allies +7/10/14/18% AS and AP; Lunar units themselves get more.
- **Primal** 2/4: choose one Primal blessing each.
- **Rapidfire** 2/3/4/5: team +10% AS; Rapidfire units gain +3/5/9/15% AS per attack (max 10 stacks).
- **Ravager** 2/4/6: 10% omnivamp, +12/25/40% damage, doubled against enemies below 50% HP.
- **Riftbeast** 3/5/7/10: 3: Alpha Mark gives one Riftbeast a unique buff; 5: every 3rd shop is flooded with Riftbeasts; 7: at combat start and every 5 s Riftbeasts grow (+5% AD/AP/AS, +5 armor/MR, +50 HP); 10: +2 team slots.
- **Rival** 1/2: works only with exactly 1 Rival fielded. Kha'Zix evolves on takedowns (choose Executioner/Rapidfire/Ravager/Spellweaver); Rengar's takedowns give gold and, past a threshold, the team gains AD. At 2 both can be fielded and buff each other.
- **Solar** 3: team shield for 5% max HP, +8% magic damage; strengthened for each distinct 3★ unit; at 3/5/8 3★ units unlocks AS + armor/MR / partial true damage / periodically upgrading a 3★ to 4★.
- **Spellweaver** 2/4/6: team +10% AP; Spellweavers +10/30/55% AP and +1/1/2% AP per cast.
- **Sprykin** 3/5/7: gain a BFF; rider +15/40/45% HP and +15/35/45% AS; at 5/7, 50/100% of the BFF's ability also applies to all Sprykins.
- **Summoner** 2/3: empowers summons (Yorick HP, Azir damage, Mama Beak +45% damage, Zyra's plants attack more); +50% effect at 3.
- **Vanguard** 2/4/6: shield for 18/30/40% max HP at combat start and on first dropping below 50% HP (10 s); at 6, +5% damage reduction while shielded.

**Unique traits**
- Apex Predator (Elder Dragon): takes 2 slots, Riftbeast +2.
- Attuned (Alune): each cast changes the moon phase; at half or less the team gets +7% damage reduction, above half +7% damage amp.
- Avatar (Lux): once you own a Lux, every other Lux in the shop has the same origin; Lux's origin counts as 2.
- Bounty Seeker (Draven): choose bounty quests; complete one for a reward, then choose the next.
- Caustic (Kog'Maw): damage reduces enemy armor/MR by 30% for 4 seconds.
- Emerald Aspect (Taric): drag an ally onto Taric to pair them; the ally gets a bonus from Taric's ability.
- Greenfather (Ivern): gather seeds to make board hexes grow bonuses.
- Monolith (Malphite): +10 armor/MR for each enemy targeting him.
- Old Growth (Maokai): permanently gains max HP when nearby enemies die.
- Thornmaiden (Zyra): team +5% damage reduction, increased when Zyra has enough plants.
