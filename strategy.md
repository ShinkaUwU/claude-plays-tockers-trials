# Fixed strategy (edit this file to change the comp)

This file holds the comp, leveling schedule, rolling rules and item assignment that the skill follows. It has the highest priority: when it conflicts with general advice in `SKILL.md`, follow this file. To play a different comp, edit only this file — the rest of the skill (input script, coordinates, pitfalls, mechanics) does not depend on it.

Card names are as shown in game; costs and traits are in `s18-reference.md`.

## Priority cards

- Yunara (2-cost, Blossom·Executioner)
- Alistar (2-cost, Elderwood·Brawler)
- LeBlanc (2-cost, Elderwood·Spellweaver)
- Azir (3-cost, Blackthorn·Executioner·Summoner)
- Sett (4-cost, Blossom·Brawler)
- Ahri (4-cost, Blossom·Spellweaver)
- Ezreal (4-cost, Elderwood·Executioner)
- Soraka (4-cost, Flora Fatalis·Executioner)
- Kennen (5-cost, Inferno·Executioner): the upgrade over Azir — once you have Kennen, he replaces Azir. **Never field Kennen and Azir together**; strip Azir's items with the Golden Item Remover that same round and reassign them by category.
- Gnar (5-cost, Elderwood·Sprykin·Brawler): the upgrade over Alistar — once you have Gnar, he replaces Alistar.
- Last two flex slots (when there are spare slots and every other planned card is fielded): pick two of Ivern (Greenfather), Taric (Emerald Aspect), Draven (Bounty Seeker).
- **Every card in the final comp has the same buy priority, flex picks included.** Whenever one appears in the shop and that unit isn't 2★ yet, buy it — even if a 1★ copy is already on the board, and including during level-10 rolls. Only skip it once the unit is 2★ (unless you are deliberately going for its 3★).

## Leveling schedule

- Level 4 at 1-4, level 5 at 1-7, level 6 at 1-9.
- 2-1: level to 7 if at least 30 gold remains afterwards; otherwise level to 7 at 2-3.
- Level 8 around 2-7, timed so 30–40 gold remains after leveling.
- After reaching level 10, don't hold gold: stop saving for interest and spend everything rolling for 2★ 5-costs (Kennen, Gnar).

## Rolling and shop wands

- **Never press reroll before level 8**, including free rerolls from orbs (save them for level 8).
- **The round you hit level 8**: buy the first wand that appears whatever it is, then roll for 2★ 4-costs (2★ Sett and one or two 2★ backline 4-costs: Ahri / Ezreal / Soraka). Stop as soon as you hit; save gold for level 9.
- **The round you hit level 9**: likewise buy the first wand whatever it is, then roll for 2★ 5-costs (Kennen, Gnar).
- **Every round after level 8**: roll until a wand appears and buy it (one wand per round is the maximum; see "Shop wands" in `SKILL.md`).
- Early on, before these cards show up, use what you have to preserve HP, but prioritize gold for the leveling schedule — don't spend gold rolling for a transitional board or buying low-cost cards that are already 2★.

## Item assignment (mechanical, no improvising)

Classify each item only as tank item, AD damage item, or AP damage item, then give it to the one corresponding unit.

1. **Fixed main tank**: Alistar until Sett arrives; Sett once he arrives; Gnar if you have Gnar. All tank items (Gargoyle Stoneplate, Sunfire Cape, Warmog's Armor, Bramble Vest, Dragon's Claw, etc.) go only to the main tank. When the main tank changes, move all tank items to the new one with the Golden Item Remover that same round.
2. **Fixed carries**: early AD carry is Yunara, early AP carry is LeBlanc. As soon as a planned 4-cost carry reaches 2★, move all damage items of that type to it that same round with the Golden Item Remover: AD items to Ezreal, AP items to Ahri or Soraka; if both Ahri and Soraka are 2★, AP items go to Soraka (move them off Ahri if they are already there).
3. **Check before Fight**: the lineup checklist names the main tank, AD carry and AP carry and their items. If the item bench still has a component that can be combined or given to the main tank / a carry, do not click Fight.
4. **Azir exception**: early on Azir may hold AP-marksman items (attack speed / AP items like Guinsoo's Rageblade, Nashor's Tooth, Jeweled Gauntlet). Once a planned 4-cost carry reaches 2★, move those items to it first (per rule 2: AD to Ezreal, AP to Soraka/Ahri). Don't build traits for Azir, don't chase Blackthorn/Summoner, and ignore the blood-pool hex.
5. **Other units get no items** unless the main tank and the carries already have 3 items each.
